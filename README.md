## Selected Projects

**[QuickFuel](https://github.com/RushdiNura/quick-fuel)** · [Live demo](https://quick-fuel-navy.vercel.app/)
Fuel reservation for drivers and station operations for owners and operators: weekly quotas, hourly pickup slots, pickup codes and two-step dispensing. React, TypeScript and Supabase (Postgres with row-level security, scheduled jobs and edge functions).

**[HR Reporting System](https://github.com/RushdiNura/hr-report-system)** · [Live demo](https://hr-report-system-frontend.vercel.app/)
Department coordinators submit structured reports and HR reviews totals on a dashboard. JWT authentication, role-based access control, REST APIs, and DOCX and Excel export.

**[Aerlume Travel Booking](https://github.com/RushdiNura/travel-booking-system)** · [Live demo](https://aerlume-travel-booking.vercel.app/)
Flights, hotels and holiday packages booked against managed inventory, with Stripe payments, separate customer and admin dashboards, and an AI trip planner.

**[JobNest](https://github.com/RushdiNura/job-portal-website-MERN)** · [Live demo](https://rushdi-job-board-website.vercel.app/)
A job portal for job seekers and employers with role-based dashboards, applications, resume uploads and applicant management.

More, with write-ups of the engineering decisions and open items for each, on my **[portfolio](https://portfolio-react-phi-self.vercel.app/)**.
